<h5><strong>School Logo</strong></h5>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 90%;" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/Spectra_Logo_Schoolname.png">
</div>
<p>Spectra in Latin means Spectrum and it encapsulates our philosophy of
recognising the different strengths and talents of our students.</p>
<p>The School logo features a star that radiates a band of colours emanating
from a single light source. The emerging colours symbolise the multiple
streams of opportunities offered by the School. The radiating lines reflect
the School’s recognition that each student’s learning progress will be
different.</p>
<p><strong>Dusk Purple</strong> represents the School’s commitment to nurture
our students to grow and shine. <strong>Pink</strong> represents compassion,
love and kindness, while <strong>Green</strong> represents a vibrant and
positive attitude towards life.</p>
<p>The angled lines converging towards the centre depict a strong community
spirit resembling the kampung or “village”, and a reminder that <strong>“It takes a village to raise a child”.</strong>
</p>
<p>In essence, it represents the School’s commitment to provide multiple
platforms to help every student shine to the brightest of their potential.</p>

<h5><strong>School Motto</strong></h5>

<p>The motto “<strong>Together We Can</strong>” is the School’s rallying
call. It serves to remind every Spectran that with collective effort and
steadfast faith, we can achieve success.</p>
